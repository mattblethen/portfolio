## Overview

The latest Castle Jewelry update pushed the custom Shopify theme much further: more flexible merchandising sections, stronger promotional controls, cleaner mobile navigation, and a fresh round of performance tuning aimed squarely at Lighthouse and Core Web Vitals.

The storefront now gives the team more control over featured collections, campaigns, and product storytelling without sacrificing speed. The result is a sharper shopping experience that feels more premium on the front end and more maintainable behind the scenes.

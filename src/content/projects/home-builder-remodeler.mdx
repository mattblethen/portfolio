## Overview

**Northline Design Build** is a ready-to-own demo site for a custom home builder, remodeler, or design-build firm that needs to feel established from day one.

The concept leans into a warmer, premium visual language: large project imagery, detail-forward copy, and a layout built around trust, process, and local-service credibility. It is meant to be easy to adapt for businesses focused on custom homes, kitchen and bath remodels, additions, or higher-end renovations.

Highlights:

- **Project-led homepage** that quickly shows quality and craftsmanship
- **Service page structure** built for custom homes, remodels, additions, and renovations
- **Process and about content** that supports higher-consideration buying decisions
- **Contact page** with strong estimate CTA and clear local-service framing
- **Reskin-friendly design system** that can be updated quickly with a real brand's logo, palette, photos, and copy

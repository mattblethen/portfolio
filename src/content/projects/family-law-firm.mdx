## Overview

**Bluegrass Hearth Family Law** is a ready-to-own starter site for a family law attorney or small firm that needs to feel steady, capable, and established from the first screen.

The concept is built around a calmer visual language than most legal sites: warmer copy, stronger hierarchy, and trust-building pages that help prospective clients understand what happens next before they ever pick up the phone. It is designed to reskin easily for a real practice serving divorce, custody, mediation, support, and related family-law matters.

Highlights:

- **Consultation-first homepage** with clear local-service positioning and trust-heavy messaging
- **Practice area page structure** built for divorce, custody, mediation, support, and agreements
- **About and process pages** that reduce anxiety and set expectations clearly
- **Contact page** centered on a calm first-conversation CTA instead of hard-sell legal copy
- **Reskin-friendly design system** that can be updated quickly with a real attorney bio, photos, logo, and service area

## Overview

**Bluegrass Comfort Co.** is a ready-to-own starter site for a heating and air company that needs to feel local, responsive, and trustworthy from the first screen.

The concept is built for the kind of homeowner who is usually searching with a problem already happening: no cooling, no heat, uneven rooms, or a system they do not trust anymore. The design leans into a practical local-service tone, with strong call-first CTAs, emergency repair entry points, and a maintenance-plan page that helps turn one-time service work into recurring revenue.

Highlights:

- **Call-first homepage** with emergency repair framing and clear service-area messaging
- **Service page structure** built for repair, installation, tune-ups, and maintenance plans
- **About page** that gives the brand a small-team local-service personality
- **Contact page** designed for fast inquiry starts when a homeowner needs help quickly
- **Reskin-friendly design system** that can be updated quickly with a real HVAC company logo, phone number, photos, and service area
